## Important interpretation limitations

Creation timestamps may be absent or filesystem-dependent; POSIX `ctime` is not creation. Modification timestamps record last modification, not the full history of edits. Cumulative counts grouped by a file's current creation or modification date are not observed historical snapshot growth; use actual snapshots for growth claims. Metadata duplicate candidates are not identity proof; inferred top-level project groupings are not verified projects. Inaccessible and excluded items introduce selection bias. Imported datasets must disclose missing folders/fields, timezone and collection methodology.
